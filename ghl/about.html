About

I’m Rich Bonn.

I help self-employed people get mortgages when their tax returns say no.

Professional headshot of Rich Bonn goes here

Business owners. Entrepreneurs. People who make real money, own real businesses, have real assets… and still get treated like a financial crime scene by traditional lending.

That problem bothered me enough that I wrote the book on it: How to Get a Mortgage When Your Tax Return Says No.

I have spent 30+ years helping thousands of people buy homes, build wealth, and find financing when the Beige Box says no. Sometimes the whole problem is that nobody bothered to ask the right questions. That’s my superpower. Not memorizing guidelines. Asking better questions.

Because you built a business. You took risks. You created income your own way. Your mortgage strategy should probably acknowledge that.

I help people use real estate and smart financing to build wealth, freedom, and something worth passing down.

Where I started

I started selling at six years old at the Orange Swap Meet with my grandfather, hawking brushes, combs, and gloves. It taught me something I still use today: listen long enough and people will tell you what they actually need. The products changed. The philosophy didn’t. Today I work primarily with people throughout Greater Houston, because this is our community.

When I’m not doing mortgages

I teach CE classes for Realtors, volunteer in Houston, dispatch for Hatzalah of Houston, hang out with my wife and four dogs, and tell jokes that probably should have been declined by underwriting.

I was born in Southern California because of my parents’ poor geographic planning. People ask what brought me to Texas. A Ford Expedition.

If you are self-employed, own a business, or somebody told you your income is “too complicated”… good. Complicated is interesting. Mortgages are boring. Your loan officer shouldn’t be.

How we work

  • We keep the promise, even when it costs us something.
  • Good enough is not the standard. If a lender’s first answer is no, we ask what was really behind it.
  • Showing up consistently is just good business. Never a week without a call from us.
  • We would rather tell you no with context than keep you on a long maybe.

The squad

The people who keep your file moving.

  • Nicole Murr

    Production Partner

    Nicole has been in the mortgage industry for over 20 years. That's two decades of loan files, tight timelines, and clients who needed someone in their corner when the process got complicated. She's still here. Still answering questions. Still making sure things don't fall through the cracks.

    As Production Partner, Nicole keeps the machinery running. While Rich is mapping out your loan strategy, she's the one making sure every piece of the file is in place, every deadline is met, and you're never left wondering what's happening or what comes next.

    She's also a firm believer that no question is too small. Mortgage paperwork is confusing by design. Nicole's job is to make it less so. Twenty years in, she still picks up the phone.

    When she's not at her laptop, Nicole is chaos coordinator to five kids, partnering with her husband Justin to raise kind, capable, and compassionate humans. She can usually be found on a volleyball court, football field, or soccer sideline, laptop still open, because the loan process doesn't check the game schedule.

  • Stacy Hunjadi

    Processor

    Stacy has been in mortgages since 1992, which means she's seen just about every way a loan can go sideways. And she knows how to get it back on track.

    Processing, underwriting, funding, closing, complicated files, weird conditions, last-minute surprises. Stacy knows the game and knows how to get stuff done.

    She's calm under pressure, fiercely detail-oriented, and genuinely cares about the people behind the paperwork. When your mortgage gets complicated, Stacy is exactly who you want in your corner.
