The mismatch

Two documents. Two very different stories.

On paper

The tax return

Built to lower what you owe. Smart tax strategy can make a profitable business look small on paper.

VS

In the bank

The bank statements

Show the deposits that really came in. For many self-employed borrowers, they tell the fuller cash-flow story.

A bank statement loan adds up deposits over a set period (many programs use 12 or 24 months), removes what is not income, adjusts for business expenses, and works out a monthly figure a lender can evaluate. The details depend on the program and the file.

How it works

Four steps. No mystery.

  1. Tell us your situation

    A conversation, not a form. We start with what your business or income actually looks like.

  2. Share the documents that tell the story

    Business or personal bank statements, depending on the program. Many programs use 12 or 24 months. We tell you exactly what is needed and how to send it securely, after we talk.

  3. We run the numbers first

    You get a verified prequalification built around the income and documentation you actually have, before you fall in love with a house.

  4. Every week, we call with a purpose

    What changed, what it means, and what comes next. If the answer is not yet, you get the plan. If it is no, you get the reason and the next move.
