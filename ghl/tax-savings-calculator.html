How this works

  • The gap. We work out the monthly payment, divide by the debt-to-income limit, and get the yearly income a tax-return lender would need to see. The gap is that number minus the income on your return.
  • The cost of closing it. To show that much more income, you would give up about that much in deductions. We multiply the gap by a rough combined tax rate to estimate the extra tax.
  • The other side. A bank statement loan skips that step, and may carry a higher rate. We show the added yearly cost, and how long it takes to catch up with the one-time taxes.
