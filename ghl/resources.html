Why can a tax return and bank statements tell different stories?

A tax return is built to lower what you owe, so smart deductions can shrink the income it shows. Bank statements show deposits that actually arrived. A lender who reads only the return can miss the cash-flow picture. Bank statement programs read the documents that fit the business.

What is the difference between prequalification and preapproval?

We use verified prequalification: a review of your real documents before you shop. Some programs or legal contexts use the word preapproval, and we use it only where it applies.

What does a lender look at beyond income?

Credit, assets and reserves, the property, and how the loan is structured. Income is one piece.

What are closing costs?

Lender fees, title and settlement charges, an appraisal, and prepaid items like taxes and insurance. Your Loan Estimate lists them.

How much should I put down?

It depends on the program, the file, and the property. We show you the numbers before you write an offer, and the payment calculator shows how down payment moves the monthly figure.

Glossary

Terms we explain the first time and use plainly after that.
TermWhat it means
Bank statement loanA mortgage that works out income from deposits in your bank accounts over a set period, often 12 or 24 months, instead of relying on a tax return alone.
Beige BoxOur name for generic checkbox thinking that mistakes one document for your whole financial story.
Expense factorThe share of business deposits treated as expenses when income is calculated from bank statements. It varies by program.
HELOCHome equity line of credit. A different product from a mortgage. See our sister site, Houston Bank Statement HELOC.
LTVLoan-to-value. The loan amount divided by the property value.
Non-QMA loan that does not meet the standard Qualified Mortgage rules. Many bank statement loans are Non-QM.
PITIAPrincipal, interest, taxes, insurance, and association dues. The full monthly housing payment.
ReservesCash or liquid assets left after closing, often measured in months of payments.
Verified prequalificationA prequalification we back with a review of your actual documents.
