Realtor partners

Teach your clients one sentence.

You do not need a mortgage degree to spot a client with a Beige Box problem. You need to hear the signal and know where to send them.

The signal phrases

  • "I own my business."
  • "My income changes month to month."
  • "I write off a lot."
  • "My tax return doesn't show what I really make."
  • "A lender already told me no."

What happens after you send someone

We start with the client's situation, not a program list. We ask for the smallest useful piece of information, like two months of business bank statements. Then we run the numbers and give them a verified prequalification they can shop with. Every week, we call the client with a purpose: what changed, what it means, and what comes next.

If a bank statement loan is not the right fit, we say so. Your client's best answer is the goal.
